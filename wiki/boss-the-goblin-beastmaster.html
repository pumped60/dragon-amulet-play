The Goblin Beastmaster

The Goblin Beastmaster
Order1 of 8
Health900 (bear 900)
Damage22, backstab 48
Soldiers needed3
WhereForest warcamp

The Goblin Beastmaster is boss 1 on the ladder.

How it fights

Dash, slam and sweep with his scarred bear; slips into the shadows (unseen and untouchable for 2 seconds) and strikes from behind

Reward

Gold bars, rubies, taming whistle; a trophy of him and his bear

Bring an army

With fewer soldiers or fighting pets near it than it needs, only a small part of your damage gets through (15% alone) and it heals as the fight goes on. Every boss enrages at half health.
