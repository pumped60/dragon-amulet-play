Storm hammer
TypeTool
Tier6 of 8
Work speed4.97x
Sells for480 coins

Storm hammer is a tool (tier 6 of 8 in its upgrade line). Breaks back walls. Tap a placed block to reshape it.

How to get it

  • Craft: Storm bar ×12 + Oak planks ×3 + Sticks ×2 → Storm hammer at Workbench
  • Buy from the Blacksmith: 2401 coins

Upgrade line

Owning a better tier replaces the one before it.
