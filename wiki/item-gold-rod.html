Gold rod

Gold rod
TypeTool
Tier4 of 5
Fishingbetter catches at higher tiers
Sells for97 coins

Gold rod is a tool (tier 4 of 5 in its upgrade line). Tuna, anglerfish and better treasure. Faster bites.

How to get it

  • Craft: Gold bar ×6 + Pearl + Sticks ×2 → Gold rod at Workbench
  • Buy from the Blacksmith: 483 coins
  • Buy from the Fisher: 483 coins

Upgrade line

Owning a better tier replaces the one before it.

  1. 1.Wooden rod
  2. 2.Silver rod
  3. 3.Iron rod
  4. 4.Gold rod
  5. 5.Mythril rod
