Diamond pickaxe is a tool (tier 5 of 8 in its upgrade line).

How to get it

  • Craft: Diamond ×10 + Oak planks ×3 + Sticks ×2 → Diamond pickaxe at Workbench
  • Buy from the Blacksmith: 1001 coins
