Fire rune is a rune. Set it in a weapon: 10% of your hits set enemies on fire at level I, up to 25% at level V. Add more fire runes to level it up.

How to get it

  • Craft: Ruby ×3 + Gold bar → Fire rune at Workbench
  • Buy from the Jeweler: 295 coins
