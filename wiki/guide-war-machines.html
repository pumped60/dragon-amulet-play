War machines

Build a siege workshop (30 planks, 6 iron bars, 20 stone at a workbench), craft machines there and use the item to deploy one; up to 6 roll along behind your army. Assign a party soldier as crew in the Army panel and it fires on its own, or press F beside it to operate it yourself. Longships carry 1 machine; warships carry 20 soldiers and 3 machines.
