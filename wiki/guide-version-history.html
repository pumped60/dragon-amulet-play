Version history

Every update, newest first. Each update adds one to the last number of the version (0.9.1, 0.9.2 …).

Beta 0.9.19

  • Phones: buttons and taps on the world stay lined up after turning the phone sideways; small buttons are easier to hit.
  • Moving items on phones: hold an item to drag it, or tap it and then tap the slot where it goes, in the bag and in chests. Tap a chest item twice to send it straight across.

Beta 0.9.18

  • Phones: the ✕ closes every menu and pop-up (pause, More, the eye list) without tapping what is under it, and item pop-ups stay up.
  • Item taps: just the name; tap the name for the picture and every stat. Settings > Screen chooses Name only, Short, Everything or Off.
  • Settings: tabs for Controls, Screen, Sound, World and Computer.
  • Screen layout: the item bar and the health, mana, XP and hunger bars can run across or up and down.

Beta 0.9.17

  • Dragon compass: found in chests (always one in the goblin warcamp's gold chest); shows which way the dragon's lair lies and how far. Without it you can't tell.
  • Astrolabe: found in chests or bought from the Jeweler; shows the season, the weather and the days until the next season. Without it the clock shows only the day.
  • Test world: starts with both.

Beta 0.9.16

  • Trees: back to their original look, with the leaves over the trunk.

Beta 0.9.15

  • Show on screen: the eye opens a list with everything ticked; untick a part to hide it at once.
  • Multiplayer: every player sees bomb blasts and boss warning marks.

Beta 0.9.14

  • Top bar: Bag (Crafting is a tab inside), Army, Buildings, God menu, Menu and ⋯ More; Speed is in the God menu; Fly sits beside Torch on phones.

Beta 0.9.13

  • Item screens: the God menu and creative All items are grids of tiles with filters; phone crafting shows two shorter recipes per row; the corner info lines are easier to read.

Beta 0.9.12

  • Settings > Torches: choose what the Torch button tap does, whether double-tap places a torch, whether tools rest with the torch on, and whether Shift lights your torch.

Beta 0.9.11

  • Phones: Stick and tap always mines the nearest block toward your tap; the Torch button lights your way, one tap places a torch where you tap, and tools rest until you switch it off.

Beta 0.9.10

  • Wiki: a page for every item, recipe, class, monster, boss, animal, shop and crafting station, linked together, with search (pumped60.github.io/dragon-amulet-play/wiki).
  • Multiplayer: Recent worlds with their codes in the Multiplayer menu.

Beta 0.9.9

  • Silver gear: a silver sword, mace, daggers, bow, staff, pickaxe, axe, hoe, hammer and fishing rod between wood and iron, each halfway between the two. Made from silver bars (silver ore, found above iron).
  • Prices: shop prices are half the value of the materials; 20% more iron ore; selling never beats buying.
  • Play: pickaxes clear plants; a double jump breaks a fall; weather can be switched off; phone move stick on the left (or right for left-handed players).
  • Multiplayer: friends see your grappling hook; arrows and spells from soldiers, enemies and friends fly the right way on every screen; world changes arrive twice as fast.
  • Dragonborn: hold B on a computer; flames stop at the ground and walls.
  • Icons: sticks, pickaxes, hoes, the silver rod, whistle, wings and amulet redrawn.

Beta 0.9.8

  • Trees: wood all the way up inside the leaves, branches with leafy clumps on big trees, cone-shaped spruces, bark showing through the leaves.
  • Multiplayer: one world for everyone: felled trees, back walls, new houses and castle floors, crops and saplings match for every player; wanderers given jobs by any player become the same villagers for all; friends' arrows fly the way they were aimed.
  • Water: falls through platforms, slides down slopes and stairs, and covers furniture standing in it; rain and floods leave half as much.
  • Pens: animals stay in until the water is three quarters of a block over the fence.
  • Light: a roof alone no longer makes it dark; only a space closed in with a back wall is dark.
  • Weather: storms and rain last 12 hours, and their water drains within the next 6.
  • Controls: mouse aim lock starts on.
  • Desktop apps: they fetch the newest game each time they start.

Beta 0.9.7

  • Menus: an X in the top right corner of every menu, always visible.
  • Phones: joysticks by default again; "Stick and tap" (move stick on the right, tap anywhere to use your item); auto sprint (Settings); double-tap to place a torch.
  • New world: all six class buttons are the same size on phones and computers; the Dragonborne weapon tally sits under them.

Beta 0.9.6

  • Desktop: install the play site as an app on a Mac or Windows computer from Chrome or Edge (it works offline), or download the Mac and Windows apps from the Releases page of pumped60/dragon-amulet-play on GitHub.

Beta 0.9.5

  • Fire: wood, leaves, plants and grass catch fire, flicker, spread a little and burn out; started by the Dragonborn, fire bombs, lava, lightning and raiders.
  • Saplings: one for every kind of tree, each growing into its own tree.
  • Phones: short item info by default (Settings: Short or Full). Test world: no opening screen; the Dragonborn starts with all his weapons. The Dragonborn has no horns.

Beta 0.9.4

  • Two-finger controls on phones: the first finger moves, the second aims and uses; the joysticks can still be picked in Settings.
  • The Dragonborn: animated red wings, a swaying tail, and a Fire dial on phones to aim his breath.

Beta 0.9.3

  • The Dragonborn: always playable in the test world; in new survival and creative games he unlocks only after you defeat Vorthax in a survival world.

Beta 0.9.2

  • The Dragonborn: a sixth class, unlocked by defeating Vorthax. He flies whenever he likes, breathes fire (hold B, or the Breath button on phones) and is the strongest class.
  • Dragonborne weapons: Vorthax drops the dragonborne weapon of your class and unlocks it; a new Dragonborn starts with every one unlocked. Collect all five by beating Vorthax with each class.

Beta 0.9.1

  • Bombs: small, fire, sticky and big bombs; all break blocks, which burst apart in tumbling pieces like a falling tree. Some pieces land as rubble that crumbles away after 3 days; the blocks around a blast are burnt (scorched stone, ash, charred wood).
  • The Demolitionist: moves into his own house in your village once the Goblin Beastmaster is beaten, and sells bombs.
  • Shop levels: every shopkeeper's shop goes from level 1 to 3 (talk to them to upgrade), each level selling better goods; level 2 needs a Town, level 3 a City.

Beta 0.9.0

  • Test world tools: the test world starts with a prepped farm a short walk from where you begin. The God menu has a World tab: build another prepped farm, ripen crops, skip to the next morning, jump to the test farm, set the weather (natural, clear, rain, thunderstorm, snowstorm) and its strength, and start a flash flood, a lightning strike or dry up rain water right away.
  • Trees: leaves stay in front of the trunk, standing and falling; a felled tree tips over until it touches the first solid block and breaks apart there; more detailed leaves and bark.
  • Land: rolling hills, slanted tunnels, sinkholes and ravines; smooth natural slopes; soft mist clouds low over the islands.
  • Birds of prey: hawks and eagles by day, owls by night; birds land now and then. Tame a landed one with raw meat (small birds take wheat seeds).
  • Farming: feed penned animals by clicking them with food; pumpkins are placeable blocks, cut into seeds, carve into jack-o'-lanterns and grow wild in the dark forest.
  • Water and plants: rain and floods fill the inside of fences and cover plants of every kind, which come back when the water dries; torches, lanterns, lamps and campfires in the water drop as items.
  • The cold: on ice islands all year and everywhere in winter, plants out in the open die under snow and snow builds up; plants under a roof survive; wild plants sprout again in spring.
  • Farm tab: crafting has a Farm tab with all seeds and plants, fences, the jack-o'-lantern and shears. Stacked fences join into one tall post.
  • Slopes: natural slopes look like the normal grass, dirt, sand or snow block cut on a slant; no plants grow or can be planted on slopes, slabs or stairs; you glide up and down slopes smoothly instead of hopping.
  • Doorways: new villager houses, the miner's hut and buildings you place get the ground outside each door reshaped into a slope at least 5 blocks long, so you can always walk out.
  • Realm castles: the Sky Ranger's castle is a tall five-floor tower with few big rooms; the Magma Knight's keep is a wide 18-room maze on three levels with a dungeon. Rooms hide in thick white or red fog until you enter, and their guards wait until then. Existing worlds get both castles rebuilt and their lords return.
  • More sky: new worlds have a taller sky realm with lots of open air above the Sky Ranger's tower; older worlds get a slightly shorter tower with more room above it.
  • Taming and lanterns: tame any animal from about 3 blocks away, even while it runs, and flying birds too; lanterns hang only from ceilings, walls or buildings; building the miner's hut slopes the ground outside every village door.
  • Level villages: the ground between buildings in every village is flat; outside the end buildings there are 3 flat blocks, then a slope to the natural ground.
  • Grappling hooks: three tiers (10, 16 and 24 blocks); hold X (or the Hook joystick on phones, dragged to aim) and let go to toss it like a fishing cast, on a loose rope that follows it; it pulls you to a wall or ledge it hits and holds you there until you press again; if it lands on the ground you press again to reel it in. Craft one, find one in a chest, or start with one.
  • Baby animals: calves, lambs, striped piglets, chicks, bunnies, fawns, cubs, penguin chicks and wolf pups each have their own look.
  • Phones and helpers: the Torch button works like holding Shift; a small Hook joystick just left of the right stick (drag to aim, let go to throw); the game fills the whole screen on the first tap; miners run home when hit and move smoothly; felling a tree only takes its own leaves.
  • Buckets: 3 iron bars at a workbench; scoop up water and pour it out again, or pour it on lava to make obsidian.
  • Lava: flows like water but slowly, creeping a block at a time; you can swim in it slowly; jump out of it once your head is above it; build over it with blocks; small lava pools appear on the surface and underground; burns plants, wood and grass, destroys dropped items, turns to obsidian in water, and slows you like honey.
  • Faces: matching eyes in every colour, glowing eyes for magma folk, face options that line up on every class, and your hair colour on the beastmaster.
  • Bosses in order: Beastmaster, Archmage, Magma Wizard, Sky Ranger, Magma Knight, Grukk, Kraken, Vorthax, each harder and each needing soldiers (3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12, 20). The wizards fly, teleport and chase you out of their towers; the Beastmaster vanishes and backstabs; the Sky Ranger has a gale and a marked shot; the Magma Knight has molten armor and floor eruptions.
  • Thrown weapons: the Adamantite sword and Magma blade fly out about 9 blocks and back like a boomerang; the Adamantite mace is on an 8-block chain and flies where you aim when you let go; the Adamantite and Kraken daggers are thrown one after another, up to 3 at once. Thrown weapons look the same size as in your hand.
  • Trophies: boss trophies are 2 by 2 replicas of the boss, standing on two trophy shelves hung on a back wall.
  • Fast travel: a Go home button on the portal map.
  • Capture: only the people on a village's own island count toward the quarter, and it can surrender while you are away; enemy castles have no roof guards.
  • Other: hold to wind up a fishing cast; grey unexplored castle rooms; bosses wait in their chamber; the magma realm is hidden until visited; water through open doors; flash floods; rain heard indoors; steeper upgrade costs.

Beta 0.8.0

  • Furniture: chests are two blocks wide and furnaces two by two everywhere; the back wall no longer shows through their second half.
  • Storm ore: lightning on open ground leaves a hole and 3 to 5 storm ore; a storm tier between diamond and mythril, a lightning rod and a storm charm.
  • Capture: warlord dead and three quarters of a faction gone: choose to take or execute the last soldiers, and the castle is yours.
  • Castles: enemy castles have a second floor; your castle has a locked safe room; villagers come back down once raiders are gone.
  • Bosses: the sky and magma throne rooms are reached from the top floor and have guards; turret knights stand on the ground floor; the dragon stays outside during raids.
  • Other: rain water dries by the next morning; the Kraken chest opens after the Kraken.

Beta 0.7.2

  • Furnaces are one solid 2 by 2 block with no gap at the top.
  • Castle flags sit on their tower roofs.

Beta 0.7.1

  • Torches: hold Shift and click to place a torch where you point, whatever you hold.
  • Tool pictures: axes, hoes and hammers redrawn with the head across the top of the handle.
  • Phones: a Torch button: tap it, then tap where the torch goes.
  • Shears: 2 iron bars at a workbench or 40 coins from the Beast trainer; use beside a sheep for 2 to 3 wool, which grows back in a day.

Beta 0.7.0

  • Fixes: the screen no longer stays dark purple after sleeping; the title backdrop returns after saving and quitting; taps on phones no longer zoom the page; drag and drop swaps items in the bag and chests.
  • Test world: starts with spectator, instakill, flight and free crafting on. Starting items: weapon, pickaxe, axe first; the recall mirror in slot 9. Spears are gone; old ones became swords.
  • Trees and plants: trees tip over when cut and their leaves fall; leaves without a trunk fall away, even under new buildings; birches are smaller and rarer; plants break with the block under them; the axe faces the way you swing.
  • World: boulders, fallen logs, bushes and flowers on the surface and a distant tree line; mythril like cloud with light blue flecks; flickering campfires; ruins lit by day; full 2 by 2 furnaces.
  • Your castle: twice as tall, with the portal, a throne and beds upstairs, furniture in the great hall and arrow slits at soldier posts. Villagers hide upstairs from raiders. Hire woodcutters and farmers; boss and warlord kills raise what every villager brings in.
  • Factions: surrender when their warlord falls with few soldiers left (the rest join you); raids burn and blast buildings; they take the goblin and orc islands after clearing them; their workers climb out of holes and light tunnels; they keep the coins from monsters they kill alone.
  • Bosses: real thrones, distinct sky castle and magma keep with dark rooms, new moves for the Sky Ranger, Magma Knight, Archmage and Magma Wizard, Grukk's war maul and Kraken fang daggers, and tokens to call bosses back. Wizard towers gain guard towers; knights below, wizards above.
  • The dragon: embers fill his cave, his gold is one heap you scoop up, and every 5th day he may raid a village for a minute.
  • New gear: 15 charms (one worn at a time), 5 boat upgrades, sharks and seasonal rare fish, a fishing line that casts. Weather strength is chosen per world: lightning fires, puddles that drain, snow that settles and melts, rain that stops at your roof.

Beta 0.6.1

  • Enemy colonies: enemy villages work like colonies. Near you, woodcutters fell real trees and plant saplings, quarrymen dig a widening pit, farmers harvest and replant, and every load is carried home. Builders put up houses, farms, palisade walls, castle expansions and watchtowers one block at a time. Far away, the same work goes on in the background.
  • Growing villages: each new enemy house (up to 4) brings a villager, room for one more worker and 3 more soldiers.
  • Phones: tapping the hotbar shows the item name for 3 seconds; tapping an item in the bag or a chest shows a pop-up with its full stats for 3 seconds.
  • Growing your village: the Quartermaster in your castle offers it too, and a hint after you claim your village tells you where.

Beta 0.6.0

  • War at sea: enemy armies march to the shore, launch a rowboat, longship or warship, sail across, land and march on, island by island. Far from you the trip still happens, at marching and sailing speed, so an attack takes a few minutes and can be met at sea. Island expeditions sail too. You are warned when an army lands on your island.
  • Naval battles: enemy ships can be sunk (coins, sometimes gold and iron bars; the crew is left in the water). Their archers and mages shoot back, and warships fire cannons. Sail within 170 blocks of an enemy island and the faction sends a patrol ship after you.
  • Deck cannons I to III: your longships and warships fire cannonballs at enemy ships, sea monsters and enemies near the water (warships fire two). Crafted at a siege workshop; tier I is also sold by the shipwright.
  • Enemy workers carry a sack of wood or stone home to their castle; near you, the faction only earns it when they deliver it.
  • Multiplayer: the host's enemies fight guests' soldiers and fighting pets. Guests can save the shared world (Friend's world, slot 5), and keep playing their copy if the host leaves.
  • Village growth: hamlet, town, city. Each tier adds a house and room for more soldiers; a town brings a jeweler, a city the war engineer and warships at the shipwright.
  • Loyalty: conquered villages grow loyal (tribute and soldiers) or restless (rebellion).
  • Runes for weapons, veteran soldiers with ranks and traits, a bestiary with damage bonuses, timed expeditions, seasons and weather (winter freezes the sea), and boss trophies to mount at home.
  • Doors in spruce, birch, palm, jungle and dark oak.
  • Archer balance: bows hit 30% harder for archers, archer arrows pass through a second enemy, archers have 100 health and 2 armor, and the short bow does 12 damage.
  • Test world: pick any class first.
  • Sound: no burst of hit sounds when a world loads; sounds fade with distance; fighting music starts and stops smoothly.

Beta 0.5.1

  • Sky realm: the cloud islands sit closer together, with small cloud stepping stones between them (added to existing worlds too).
  • Mythril ore: now a pale teal cloud block that blends in with the clouds, and its veins are three times bigger (6 to 12 blocks). Veins that already formed in older worlds grow to the new size when the world loads.
  • Magma realm: twice as wide (half the world's width) in new worlds, with many more adamantite veins (4 to 8 blocks each). Existing adamantite veins grow when an older world loads.
  • Realm boss upgrades: the Sky Ranger and the Magma Knight each drop two top permanent upgrades you don't have yet: Swiftness elixir III first, then Sprint elixir III, then a Heart of vigor and a Heart of the arcane.
  • Test world: you always have a recall mirror (given when the test world starts and whenever you load it without one).

Beta 0.5.0

  • Version: the title and pause screens show the Beta version.
  • Bag and chests: the bag holds the hotbar plus 6 rows of 9 (63 slots); chests hold 9 rows of 9 (81). Drag an item out of the bag window, or onto the Drop box beside Delete, to drop it on the ground. Hover over any slot (hotbar, bag, chest) to see the item.
  • Gear locker: a permanent section in the bag keeps tools, weapons and upgrades without using bag space; move them between the locker and the bag at any time. The bag no longer lists "new items"; your upgrades always show and only change when an item is dropped, destroyed or put in a chest.
  • Crafting and shops: Craft All makes as many as you can afford; hold a Craft, Buy or Sell button to repeat it. Every villager buys the goods of their trade (the herbalist flowers, seeds and crops; the blacksmith ores, stones, bars and gems, and so on).
  • Placing stations: furnaces, workbenches, chests and other big stations show their full footprint before you place them, like the building menu.
  • Chests underwater: chests can sit underwater and water flows around them. Chests and furnaces in older buildings were rebuilt to the right size.
  • Torches: a torch can replace grass, flowers and crops (the plant drops its seeds or item; trees are not replaced). Held or Shift torches give no light underwater.
  • Taming whistle: now a permanent upgrade shown with your gear (80 coins at the beast trainer, or crafted). Any class can tame with it by feeding 1 food to a small animal, 2 to a medium one and 3 to a big one. Beastmasters tame without any food.
  • Farms and pens: 4 or more fences in a row on the ground form a pen. Give a tamed animal the Farm job in the Army panel and it settles into the nearest pen; walking into a pen with animals following you asks whether they should stay. Penned animals produce goods every morning and cannot be hurt. Fences are drawn in front of you and your animals.
  • Combat: enemy fighters flank you, archers pick off the weakest target, wounded commanders call their soldiers, and every boss enrages at half health and calls for help (Grukk also slams the ground). Orc crossbowmen now carry crossbows, orc cutthroats daggers and orc brutes warhammers. The ogre hit flash is gone.
  • Factions: enemy factions only build towers on open ground at least 10 blocks from any building made with the world. Their towers have two windows on every floor. Clearing the guards of an enemy outpost or keep captures it. Conquering a castle frees its prisoner automatically.
  • Villager tasks: your shopkeepers offer delivery tasks for coins (up to 3 at a time).
  • Ores: mythril only forms in the sky realm's clouds after the Sky Ranger falls; adamantite only in the magma realm after both realm bosses fall. Chests no longer hold mythril or adamantite bars.
  • Biomes: snow islands have no sand, and deserts never overlap ice, forest or orc islands.
  • Respawning: you and every soldier and pet who respawns with you come back with full health and a full hunger bar.
  • Boats: riders sit and row instead of running.
  • Menus: villager and chest windows close when you walk more than 10 blocks away.
  • Fixes: islands no longer turn black after saving and loading, night monsters spawn again after a reload, and the game no longer freezes when soldiers on a raft fight sea creatures.
  • Ending: slaying Vorthax plays an epilogue and credits; then keep playing, or start New Game Plus with your character in a new world where enemies are 50% tougher for each New Game Plus.
  • Multiplayer: friends who join get the host's real world (castles, doors and buildings as the host sees them), see and use portals, see each other's swings, rowing and other motions, share chest contents, give items to anyone within 5 blocks (F, or Give in the bag), come back where they left off, and can jump to a friend by tapping them on the map or tapping the arrow that points at them.
