Crops and growth

CropGrowing speedStage 2 breakStage 3 harvest (hoe)
WheatFast1 wheat seed2-3 wheat + 1-2 seeds
CarrotsMedium1 carrot seed2-3 carrots, maybe a seed
PotatoesMedium-slow1 seed potato2-4 potatoes + 1-2 seed potatoes
PumpkinsSlow1 pumpkin seed1 pumpkin + 1-2 seeds (wild ones grow in the forest)

Stage 1 gives nothing. Growth dust (herbalist) grows a plant a stage and makes it grow 50% faster. Placing a torch on a crop or flower replaces it and drops its seeds or item.
