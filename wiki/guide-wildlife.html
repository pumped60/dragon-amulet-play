Wildlife

Green animals never attack, yellow ones fight back when hit, red ones hunt you; bears can be either. Any animal except birds can be tamed: Beastmasters need no food, other classes need the taming whistle and 1, 2 or 3 food by size. Tamed animals can be pets (cannot be hurt), fighters (gone for good if they die), gatherers or farm animals (they live in a fence pen and produce goods each morning).
