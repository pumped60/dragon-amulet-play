Use a rune to set it into one of your weapons. It stays with that weapon line through every upgrade. Each rune has five levels: add more of the same rune to level it up (2 more runes for level II, 3 for III, 4 for IV and 5 for V, 15 runes in all). Setting a different rune replaces the old one and starts again at level I.
