Dragonborn

Dragonborn
Health220
Mana160
Armor9
Starting weaponIron sword

Dragonborn — Half dragon: flies whenever he likes and breathes fire (hold B on a computer, or the Fire dial on phones). The most health, armor and mana of any class. Starts with every dragonborne weapon you have unlocked.

Unlocking

Always playable in the test world. For new survival and creative games it unlocks on this device after you defeat Vorthax in survival. He flies (G on a computer), breathes fire (hold B, or the Fire dial on phones) and starts with every dragonborne weapon unlocked so far:

  • Dragonborne sword
  • Dragonborne bow
  • Dragonborne mace
  • Dragonborne staff
  • Dragonborne daggers
