The Magma Wizard

The Magma Wizard
Order3 of 8
Health4,000
Damage30 per bolt, burns
Soldiers needed5
WhereTop of the magma fortress, with imps and magma knights

The Magma Wizard is boss 3 on the ladder.

How it fights

Flies, teleports and chases like the Archmage; burning bolts, meteors and fire from the ground

Reward

Magma staff; the magma portal opens at the top of the fortress

Bring an army

With fewer soldiers or fighting pets near it than it needs, only a small part of your damage gets through (15% alone) and it heals as the fight goes on. Every boss enrages at half health.
