Dragon Amulet

A 2D sandbox adventure by Kinard Sproles. Free to play.

A dragon named Vorthax sleeps on a pile of gold, and the islands are full of monsters, rival armies and fearsome bosses. Start as a lone hero with a ruined village, grow it into a city, raise an army, and lead them into battle against the dragon.

🛡️ Choose your hero

Be an intrepid Knight with sword and shield, a quick Archer, a hard-hitting Macebearer, a Wizard who casts magic, or a Beastmaster who tames wild animals to fight beside you.

⛏️ Mine and craft

Dig deep through stone, gem caves and glowing caverns for iron, gold, diamonds and rarer ores, then forge better weapons, armor and tools.

🏰 Build up your village

Rebuild the abandoned village, then grow it into a town and a city with shops, farms, woodcutters and miners working for you. Build towers, walls and your own designs.

⚔️ Raise an army

Hire knights, archers, wizards and more, train them, set them in formations and post them in your towers. Bring war machines like ballistas and trebuchets.

🔥 Fight enemies and bosses

Hold off raids on your village and take the fight to rival factions. Defeat the Goblin Beastmaster, the Archmage, Grukk the Ogre King, the Kraken and the masters of the sky and magma realms.

🐉 Slay the dragon

When your army is ready, march on Vorthax's lair. Win, and you unlock the Dragonborn: a hero who flies and breathes fire. Play alone or with friends online.

📱 Phone or tablet

Nothing to download from a store: open the game in the browser once and put its icon on your Home Screen. It then opens full screen like an app and works offline.

iPhone or iPad
  1. Open Safari and go to the game: pumped60.github.io/dragon-amulet-play
  2. Tap Share (the square with an arrow; on newer iPhones tap ••• first)
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add
  4. Start the game from the new icon and turn the phone sideways
Android
  1. Open Chrome and go to the game: pumped60.github.io/dragon-amulet-play
  2. Tap the three dots at the top right
  3. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen), then Install
  4. Start the game from the new icon and turn the phone sideways
