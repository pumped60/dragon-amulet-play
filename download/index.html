📱 Phone or tablet

Nothing to download from a store: open the game in the browser once and put its icon on your Home Screen. It then opens full screen like an app and works offline.

iPhone or iPad
  1. Open Safari and go to the game: pumped60.github.io/dragon-amulet-play
  2. Tap Share (the square with an arrow; on newer iPhones tap ••• first)
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add
  4. Start the game from the new icon and turn the phone sideways
Android
  1. Open Chrome and go to the game: pumped60.github.io/dragon-amulet-play
  2. Tap the three dots at the top right
  3. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen), then Install
  4. Start the game from the new icon and turn the phone sideways
📄
The same steps, in more detail, are in the install guide (PDF).
